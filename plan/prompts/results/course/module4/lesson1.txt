---
title: Lesson 1 - Sentence Structure — [Subject] は [Object] を [Verb]
weight: 1
---
type: explanation
The most common Japanese sentence pattern is: [topic]は [object]を [verb]. The verb always comes last. This is the opposite of English word order.
---
type: sentence_structure
text: わたしはみずをのみます
romanji: Watashi wa mizu wo nomimasu
word: わたし
romanji: watashi
meaning: I
pos: pronoun
word: は
romanji: wa
meaning: (topic marker)
pos: particle
word: みず
romanji: mizu
meaning: water
pos: noun
word: を
romanji: wo
meaning: (object marker)
pos: particle
word: のみます
romanji: nomimasu
meaning: drink
pos: verb
