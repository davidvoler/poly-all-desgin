---
title: Lesson 7 - Sentence Structure — も (also) in Full Sentences
weight: 7
---
type: explanation
も replaces は to say "also" or "too". When the same action applies to two people, use AもBも to say "both A and B". The rest of the sentence stays the same.
---
type: sentence_structure
text: わたしもまいにちコーヒーをのみます
romanji: Watashi mo mainichi kōhī wo nomimasu
word: わたし
romanji: watashi
meaning: I
pos: pronoun
word: も
romanji: mo
meaning: also / too
pos: particle
word: まいにち
romanji: mainichi
meaning: every day
pos: adverb
word: コーヒー
romanji: kōhī
meaning: coffee
pos: noun
word: を
romanji: wo
meaning: (object marker)
pos: particle
word: のみます
romanji: nomimasu
meaning: drink
pos: verb
---
type: single_selection
text: わたしもまいにちコーヒーをのみます
