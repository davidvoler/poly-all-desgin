---
title: Lesson 6 - Sentence Structure — Possession の in Full Sentences
weight: 6
---
type: explanation
Possession の slots naturally into longer sentences. サラのねこは means "Sara's cat (is the topic)". の connects the owner to the thing before は picks up the topic.
---

type: single_selection
text: サラのねこはいえでみずをのみます
romanji: Sara no neko wa ie de mizu wo nomimasu

option: Sara drinks water with her cat at home
