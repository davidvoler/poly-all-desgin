---
title: Lesson 9 - Sentence Structure — Adjectives with です
weight: 9
---
type: explanation
Adjectives in Japanese come before the noun they describe, or after は with です. Example: かわいいねこ (cute cat) or ねこはかわいいです (the cat is cute). です makes the sentence polite.
---

romanji: Neko wa kawaii desu
