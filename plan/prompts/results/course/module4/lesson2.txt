---
title: Lesson 2 - Sentence Structure — [Subject] は まいにち [Object] を [Verb]
weight: 2
---
type: explanation
Adding a time word like まいにち (every day) or あさに (in the morning) before the object tells us when the action happens. The verb still comes last.
---
type: sentence_structure
text: わたしはまいにちみずをのみます
romanji: Watashi wa mainichi mizu wo nomimasu
word: わたし
romanji: watashi
meaning: I
pos: pronoun
word: は
romanji: wa
meaning: (topic marker)
pos: particle
word: まいにち
romanji: mainichi
meaning: every day
pos: adverb
word: みず
romanji: mizu
meaning: water
pos: noun
word: を
romanji: wo
meaning: (object marker)
pos: particle
word: のみます
romanji: nomimasu
meaning: drink
pos: verb
---
type: single_selection
text: わたしはまいにちみずをのみます
romanji: Watashi wa mainichi mizu wo nomimasu
option: I drink water every day
correct: true
option: I drink water in the morning
option: I want water every day
option: Every day I want water
