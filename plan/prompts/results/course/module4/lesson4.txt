---
title: Lesson 4 - Sentence Structure — [Subject] は [Place] に いきます
weight: 4
---
type: explanation
To say someone goes somewhere, use: [topic]は [place]に いきます. Note the difference: で marks where an action happens, に marks where someone is going.
