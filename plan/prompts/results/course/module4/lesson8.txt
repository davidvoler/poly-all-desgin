---
title: Lesson 8 - Sentence Structure — Questions with か
weight: 8
---
type: explanation
Any statement can become a question by adding か at the end. The word order stays exactly the same — Japanese does not invert the sentence to form a question.
---
type: sentence_structure
text: サラはがっこうにいきますか
romanji: Sara wa gakkō ni ikimasu ka
word: サラ
romanji: Sara
meaning: Sara
pos: proper noun
word: は
romanji: wa
meaning: (topic marker)
pos: particle
word: がっこう
romanji: gakkō
meaning: school
pos: noun
word: に
romanji: ni
meaning: to (direction)
pos: particle
word: いきます
romanji: ikimasu
meaning: go
pos: verb
word: か
romanji: ka
meaning: ? (question marker)
pos: particle
---
type: single_selection
text: サラはがっこうにいきますか
romanji: Sara wa gakkō ni ikimasu ka
option: Does Sara go to school?
correct: true
option: Sara goes to school
option: Sara goes to school, doesn't she?
option: Where does Sara go to school?
