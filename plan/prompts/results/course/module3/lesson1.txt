---
title: Lesson 1 - The Particle は (wa) — Topic Marker
weight: 1
---
type: explanation
は (wa) marks the topic of the sentence — the thing being talked about. Everything after は says something about that topic.
---
type: single_selection
text: ねこはかわいい
romanji: Neko wa kawaii
option: The cat is cute
correct: true
option: I like cats
option: Is the cat cute?
option: The cat is not cute
---
type: single_selection
text: みずはつめたい
romanji: Mizu wa tsumetai
option: The water is cold
correct: true
option: I want cold water
option: Is the water cold?
option: Cold water, please
---
type: single_selection
text: サラはがくせいです
romanji: Sara wa gakusei desu
option: Sara is a student
correct: true
option: Sara likes students
option: Is Sara a student?
option: Sara is not a student
