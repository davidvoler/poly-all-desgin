---
title: Lesson 4 - The Particle に (ni) — Direction and Time
weight: 4
---
type: explanation
に (ni) has two main uses: direction ("to" or "at" a place) and time ("at" or "on" a specific time). Example: がっこうにいく — "go to school". あさにのむ — "drink in the morning".
