---
title: Lesson 3 - The Particle を (wo) — Object Marker
weight: 3
---
type: explanation
を (wo) marks the direct object of a sentence — the thing that receives the action. It comes after the noun and before the verb. Example: みずをのむ — "drink water".
---
type: single_selection
text: みずをのむ
romanji: Mizu wo nomu

correct: true
option: want water
option: like water
option: water is cold
