---
title: Lesson 5 - The Particle の (no) — Possession
weight: 5
---
type: explanation
の (no) connects two nouns to show possession or belonging — like 's in English. Example: サラのねこ means "Sara's cat". ワタナベさんのコーヒー means "Watanabe's coffee".
---
type: single_selection
text: サラのねこ
romanji: Sara no neko
option: Sara's cat
correct: true
option: Sara likes cats
option: Sara is a cat
option: Cat named Sara

type: single_selection

romanji: Watanabe-san no kōhī

correct: true
