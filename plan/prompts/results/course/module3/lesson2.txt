---
title: Lesson 2 - The Particle か (ka) — Question Marker
weight: 2
---
type: explanation
か (ka) placed at the end of a sentence turns it into a yes/no question. No change in word order is needed — just add か at the end.
---
type: single_selection
text: ねこはかわいいですか
romanji: Neko wa kawaii desu ka
option: Is the cat cute?
correct: true
option: The cat is cute
option: The cat is not cute
option: I think the cat is cute
---
type: single_selection
text: みずはつめたいですか
romanji: Mizu wa tsumetai desu ka
option: Is the water cold?
correct: true
option: The water is cold
option: I want cold water
option: The water is not cold
---
type: single_selection
text: はい、ねこはかわいいです
romanji: Hai, neko wa kawaii desu
option: Yes, the cat is cute
correct: true
option: No, the cat is cute
option: Yes, is the cat cute?
option: Yes, I like cats
---
type: single_selection
text: いいえ、みずはつめたくないです
romanji: Iie, mizu wa tsumetakunai desu
option: No, the water is not cold
correct: true
option: Yes, the water is cold
option: No, I don't want water
option: The water is a little cold
