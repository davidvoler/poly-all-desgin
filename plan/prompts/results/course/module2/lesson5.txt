title: Lesson 5 - The Letter は (wa/ha)
weight: 5
---
type: explanation
は is normally pronounced "ha" but when used as the topic particle it is pronounced "wa". You have seen it constantly — in ねこは、みずは、コーヒーは. Now we look at the letter itself.
---
type: single_selection
text: は
romanji: ha / wa
option: the letter "ha", read as "wa" when a particle
correct: true
option: the letter "ka"
option: the letter "na"
option: the letter "a"
