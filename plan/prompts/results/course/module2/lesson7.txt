---
title: Lesson 7 - The Particle で (de) — Location of Action
weight: 7
---
type: explanation
で (de) marks the place where an action happens. Example: がっこうでべんきょうする — "study at school". It answers the question "where does this action take place?"
---
type: single_selection
text: がっこうでべんきょうする
romanji: Gakkō de benkyō suru
option: study at school
correct: true
option: go to school
option: like school
option: come from school
