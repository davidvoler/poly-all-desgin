---
title: Lesson 9 - The Particle も (mo) — Also / Too
weight: 9
---
type: explanation
も (mo) replaces は or が to mean "also" or "too". Example: わたしもコーヒーをのみます — "I also drink coffee". It adds the new topic alongside something already mentioned.
---
type: single_selection
text: わたしもコーヒーをのみます
romanji: Watashi mo kōhī wo nomimasu
option: I also drink coffee
correct: true
option: I drink coffee too much
option: I only drink coffee
option: I don't drink coffee either
