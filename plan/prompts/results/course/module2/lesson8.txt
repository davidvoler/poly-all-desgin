---
title: Lesson 8 - The Particle と (to) — And / Together With
weight: 8
---
type: explanation
と (to) has two uses: it connects nouns meaning "and" (みずとコーヒー — "water and coffee"), and it means "together with" a person (サラとべんきょうする — "study with Sara").
---
type: single_selection
text: みずとコーヒー
romanji: Mizu to kōhī
option: water and coffee
correct: true
option: water or coffee
option: water with coffee
option: water for coffee

type: single_selection

option: Sara and George are teachers
