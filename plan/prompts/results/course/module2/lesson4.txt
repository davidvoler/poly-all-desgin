title: Lesson 4 - The Letter ね (ne)
weight: 4
---
type: explanation
ね makes the sound "ne". You will see it in ねこ — the hiragana spelling of 猫 (cat). It also appears at the end of sentences as a soft "isn't it?" or "right?".
