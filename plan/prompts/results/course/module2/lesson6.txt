---
title: Lesson 6 - Particles Review — は、か、を、に、の
weight: 6
---
type: explanation
We have now seen five particles. は marks the topic. を marks the object. に marks direction or time. の shows possession. か makes a question.
---
type: single_selection
text: ねこはみずをのみます
romanji: Neko wa mizu wo nomimasu
option: The cat drinks water
correct: true
option: The cat likes water
option: Water drinks the cat
option: The cat wants water
---
type: single_selection
text: サラのねこはあさにみずをのみますか
romanji: Sara no neko wa asa ni mizu wo nomimasu ka
option: Does Sara's cat drink water in the morning?
correct: true
option: Sara's cat drinks water in the morning
option: Sara drinks water with her cat in the morning
option: Does Sara drink water in the morning?
