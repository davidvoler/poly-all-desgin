---
title: Lesson 2 - New Word — すきです (like)
weight: 2
---
type: explanation
すきです (suki desu) means "like". The thing you like is marked with が (ga) — a new particle that works like を but is used with feelings and preferences. Example: コーヒーがすきです — "I like coffee".
---
type: single_selection
text: コーヒーがすきです
romanji: Kōhī ga suki desu
option: I like coffee
correct: true
option: I drink coffee
option: I want coffee
option: Coffee is good
