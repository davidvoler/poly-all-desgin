---
title: Lesson 4 - New Word — おおきい / ちいさい (big / small)
weight: 4
---
type: explanation
おおきい (ōkii) means "big" and ちいさい (chiisai) means "small". Like かわいい and あつい, these adjectives come before the noun or after は with です.
