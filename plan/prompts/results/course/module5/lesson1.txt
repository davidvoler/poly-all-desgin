---
title: Lesson 1 - New Word — たべます (eat)
weight: 1
---
type: explanation
たべます (tabemasu) means "eat". It follows the same pattern as のみます: [topic]は [object]を たべます. The object is the food being eaten.
---
type: single_selection
text: たべます
romanji: tabemasu
option: eat
correct: true
option: drink
option: go
option: study
