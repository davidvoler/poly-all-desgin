---
title: Lesson 7 - New Word — ともだち (friend)
weight: 7
---
type: explanation
ともだち (tomodachi) means "friend". It is a noun, so it works with all the particles we know: ともだちと (with a friend), ともだちのいえ (a friend's house), ともだちがいます (there is a friend).
