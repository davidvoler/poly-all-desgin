---
title: Lesson 9 - New Word — おいしい (delicious)
weight: 9
---
type: explanation
おいしい (oishii) means "delicious" or "tasty". Like かわいい and おおきい, it is an adjective — it comes before a noun or after は with です.
---
type: single_selection
text: おいしい
romanji: oishii
option: delicious
correct: true
option: cute
option: hot
option: big
---
type: single_selection
text: コーヒーはおいしいです
romanji: Kōhī wa oishii desu
option: The coffee is delicious
correct: true
option: The coffee is hot
option: The coffee is cold
option: The coffee is good
---
type: single_selection

romanji: Gohan wa oishii desu ka
option: Is the rice delicious?
correct: true
option: The rice is delicious
option: Is the rice hot?
option: Do you like rice?
