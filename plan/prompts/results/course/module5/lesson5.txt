---
title: Lesson 5 - New Word — よみます (read)
weight: 5
---
type: explanation
よみます (yomimasu) means "read". It follows the same pattern as たべます and のみます. The thing you read is marked with を.
---
type: single_selection
text: よみます
romanji: yomimasu
option: read
correct: true
option: drink
option: eat
option: write
