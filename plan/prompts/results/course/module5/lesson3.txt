---
title: Lesson 3 - New Word — います / あります (there is)
weight: 3
---
type: explanation
います (imasu) means "there is" for living things — people and animals. あります (arimasu) means "there is" for objects. Example: ねこがいます — "there is a cat". みずがあります — "there is water".

text: ねこがいます
romanji: Neko ga imasu

word: に

word: がくせい
romanji: gakusei

pos: noun
word: が
romanji: ga

pos: particle
word: います
romanji: imasu

pos: verb
