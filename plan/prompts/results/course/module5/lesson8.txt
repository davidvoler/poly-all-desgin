---
title: Lesson 8 - New Word — いつも / ときどき (always / sometimes)
weight: 8
---
type: explanation
いつも (itsumo) means "always" and ときどき (tokidoki) means "sometimes". Like まいにち, these time adverbs slot in right after は, before the place or object.
