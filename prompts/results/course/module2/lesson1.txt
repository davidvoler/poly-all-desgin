title: Lesson 1 - The Letter あ (a)
weight: 1
---
type: explanation
あ is the first hiragana letter. It makes the sound "a" as in "father". You have already seen it in ありがとう (arigatō) and かわいい (kawaii).
---

romanji: a
option: the letter "a"
correct: true
option: the letter "i"
option: the letter "u"
option: the letter "ka"
