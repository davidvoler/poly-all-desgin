---
title: Lesson 3 - Sentence Structure — [Subject] は [Place] で [Object] を [Verb]
weight: 3
---
type: explanation
When we add a place with で, it slots in before the object. The order becomes: [topic]は [place]で [object]を [verb]. The verb is always last.
---
type: sentence_structure
text: わたしはいえでコーヒーをのみます
romanji: Watashi wa ie de kōhī wo nomimasu
word: わたし
romanji: watashi
meaning: I
pos: pronoun
word: は
romanji: wa
meaning: (topic marker)
pos: particle
word: いえ
romanji: ie
meaning: home
pos: noun
word: で
romanji: de
meaning: at (location of action)
pos: particle
word: コーヒー
romanji: kōhī
meaning: coffee
pos: noun
word: を
romanji: wo
meaning: (object marker)
pos: particle
word: のみます
romanji: nomimasu
meaning: drink
pos: verb
---
type: single_selection
text: わたしはいえでコーヒーをのみます
romanji: Watashi wa ie de kōhī wo nomimasu

correct: true
option: I drink coffee at school

option: I want coffee at home
