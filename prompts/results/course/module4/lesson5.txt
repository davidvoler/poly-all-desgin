---
title: Lesson 5 - Sentence Structure — [Subject] は [Subject2] と [Verb]
weight: 5
---
type: explanation
To say someone does something with someone else, use と before the verb. The full pattern: [topic]は [person]と [place]で [object]を [verb]. Build it up piece by piece.
---
type: sentence_structure
text: わたしはサラとがっこうにいきます
romanji: Watashi wa Sara to gakkō ni ikimasu
word: わたし
romanji: watashi
meaning: I
pos: pronoun
word: は
romanji: wa
meaning: (topic marker)
pos: particle
word: サラ
romanji: Sara
meaning: Sara
pos: proper noun
word: と
romanji: to
meaning: with
pos: particle
word: がっこう
romanji: gakkō
meaning: school
pos: noun
word: に
romanji: ni
meaning: to (direction)
pos: particle
word: いきます
romanji: ikimasu
meaning: go
pos: verb
---
type: single_selection
text: わたしはサラとがっこうにいきます
romanji: Watashi wa Sara to gakkō ni ikimasu
option: I go to school with Sara
correct: true
option: Sara and I like school
option: Sara goes to school for me
option: I go to school like Sara
